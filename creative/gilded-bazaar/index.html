The Gilded Bazaar

An AI-assisted economy and downtime companion for tabletop RPG campaigns.

Last reviewed 31 August 2026

Early development

The Gilded Bazaar is a campaign companion for tabletop RPG groups, with a focus on DM-managed shops, party purchases, approvals and shared campaign economy. Its public repository describes an early React Native and Supabase project, not a replacement for character-sheet software.

What is The Gilded Bazaar?

It is a tabletop RPG campaign companion focused on shops, party purchases, approvals and the shared economy around downtime.

Who is it for?

The project is shaped for campaign groups and Dungeon Masters who want an explicit, shared way to manage purchases and the consequences of an economy.

What is the source trail?

The public repository describes a React Native and Expo application using TypeScript and Supabase, with the project still in early development.

Current status and limits

Early development. It is not presented as a character-sheet replacement, finished service or generally available product.
