Project PlainSpeak

A diagnostic and review assistant for plain-language communication.

Last reviewed 31 August 2026

Active development

MIT-licensed public successor repository.

What is PlainSpeak?

PlainSpeak helps people understand how readable their writing is, where readers may struggle and what can reasonably be improved.

What problem does it solve?

Complex language creates barriers in medical, legal, public-service, educational and everyday writing. PlainSpeak makes those barriers inspectable.

How does it work?

The public repository describes six readability metrics, difficulty bands, barrier findings, confidence levels, glossary suggestions, accessible reports and support for common document formats.

Who is it for?

Public-service writers, healthcare communicators, educators, legal-aid organisations and anyone who wants their writing to be understood by more people.

Diagnostic, not authoritative rewriting.

Suggestions require human review, especially for legal, medical, financial or safety-critical content. The tool is described as offline and no-network by default.

FAQ

Does PlainSpeak rewrite documents automatically?

No. Its public description positions it primarily as a diagnostic and review tool. Suggestions require human review.

Does PlainSpeak send text to a cloud service?

The current public successor README describes the tool as running entirely offline with no accounts, data collection or network access required.
