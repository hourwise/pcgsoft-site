Reticle Systems

A guided software planning, inspection and launch-readiness platform for AI-assisted builders.

Last reviewed 31 August 2026

Advanced development

Public source and demo links are available below.

What is Reticle?

Reticle Systems helps builders turn an idea or existing application into a structured, inspectable and more maintainable product.

What problem does it solve?

AI-assisted builds can move quickly while leaving gaps in structure, security, accessibility, SEO or launch readiness. Reticle is intended to make those gaps visible early.

How does it work?

The public repository describes a guided intake, a rule-based pre-audit assessment and planned URL and repository scanning. Payment, backend scanning and some later phases are still marked as planned.

Who is it for?

Non-technical founders, AI-assisted builders and small software teams who want a clearer path from prototype to a safer launch.

Current status

The public demo and repository document an advanced-development product with a working intake and pre-audit surface. It is not presented as a guarantee that an application is secure or ready to launch.
