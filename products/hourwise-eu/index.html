Current public product surface: hourwiseeu.co.uk

What is HourWise EU?

HourWise EU is a driver-hours and working-time companion for professional drivers operating across the UK and EU.

What problem does it solve?

It is intended to make shift tracking, break awareness and working-time decisions easier to follow during a real working day.

How does it work?

The public description centres on shift tracking, break awareness and compliance-focused alerts. This page does not claim a particular legal interpretation or guarantee compliance.

Who is it for?

Professional drivers and the people who support transport operations in UK and EU contexts.

Current status

HourWise EU is in active development. The live product surface is the best place to check current availability and behaviour.
