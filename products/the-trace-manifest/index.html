The Trace Manifest

Follow the evidence. Understand the change.

Last reviewed 21 September 2026

Live public surface — controlled rollout

Open the public evidence and knowledge surface at thetracemanifest.com. Governed features are not assumed to be fully opened.

What is The Trace Manifest?

An evidence-linked AI intelligence platform showing what changed in AI, what is credible, what is disputed and what people should actually use.

What is publicly live?

The public evidence, knowledge, stories and briefings surface is live where currently published. It provides the accessible public explanation layer.

What remains controlled?

Governed editorial capabilities, Ask/AI capabilities, provider configuration, activation controls and production feature gates remain subject to review and controlled rollout.

Why the distinction?

A public surface can be live without every governed subsystem being fully opened. The project keeps those boundaries visible rather than implying universal production availability.

Evidence before popularity.

TRACE’s public methodology makes source class, evidence quality, corroboration, timeliness and independence visible.
