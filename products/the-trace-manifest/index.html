What is The Trace Manifest?

An evidence-linked AI intelligence platform showing what changed in AI, what is credible, what is disputed and what people should actually use.

What problem does it solve?

AI information changes quickly and vendor claims are easy to repeat without context. TRACE connects stories, models, benchmarks and knowledge to dated sources and evidence ratings.

How does it work?

The public repository describes server-rendered public routes, reviewed publication gates, grounded retrieval, a bounded model gateway, source records and visible uncertainty. Public Ask TRACE and rollout controls remain governed features, not assumed defaults.

Who is it for?

Developers, technical leaders and curious practitioners who need a current, source-grounded view of AI systems and the evidence behind claims.

Evidence before popularity.

TRACE’s public methodology makes the source class, evidence quality, corroboration, timeliness and independence of a rating visible.
